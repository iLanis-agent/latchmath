Latch math

A gate starts as a measured hole in a fence, and the arithmetic starts there too. Latch math turns a measured opening into a clear span and panel count after labeled hinge and latch gaps, and a gate's weight into an illustrative hinge division. Exact arithmetic, every borrowed number labeled - and a hard boundary on what that arithmetic can say.

Open the calculators

The two tools

  • Gate plan - the opening minus a labeled 4 cm of hinge and latch gaps becomes a clear span, a panel count and a labeled span observation.
  • Hinge arithmetic - a gate's weight divided by a labeled 20 kg works out to a hinge count and screws at 6 a hinge - illustrative division only, never a hardware selection.

Labeled norms and the boundary

Boundary: these tools cannot certify a child, pool or stair gate, and they cannot choose safety hardware. Certified hardware, the manufacturer's requirements and your local codes govern any real gate - when safety is on the line, check with a licensed professional.

Labeled norms behind the arithmetic: 2 cm a side for hinge and latch gaps, a 90 cm standard span, 20 kg a hinge and 6 screws a hinge - all illustrative, since real hardware ratings and local rules move every number.
