Latch math

A gate is a promise: it swings shut, it holds, and the latch sits where small hands cannot reach. Latch math turns a measured opening into panel widths after the hinge and latch gaps, a child's height into a reach check for the latch, and the gate's weight into a hinge and screw count. Exact arithmetic, every borrowed number labeled.

Open the calculators

The three tools

  • Gate plan - the opening minus a labeled 4 cm of hinge and latch gaps becomes a clear span, a panel count and a labeled span verdict.
  • Latch plan - child height becomes a labeled reach estimate, and the latch height becomes a margin verdict.
  • Hinge plan - gate weight at a labeled 20 kg a hinge becomes the hinge count and screws at 6 a hinge.

Labeled norms and limits

Labeled norms: 2 cm a side for hinge and latch gaps, a 90 cm standard span, reach at four-thirds of height, 20 kg a hinge, 6 screws a hinge - all illustrative, since hardware ratings and local rules move the numbers. Pool barriers, child gates and stair gates are life-safety hardware: local codes set their heights, gaps and self-closing rules, and a calculator never certifies one. Check the code, then check it again with the inspector.
